01 / The opportunity

Conversations don’t always begin with the customer.

Businesses often need to reach out: to share updates, follow up, promote offers or send timely reminders. The opportunity was to bring outbound communication into SalesIQ with the right flexibility and control.

02 / Two ways to reach out

One is personal. One is for many.

Proactive messages

Reach users based on behaviour, conditions and timing.

Broadcasts

Create and send a message to a selected audience across channels.

05 / The core decisions

Compose. Target. Schedule. Route.

The outbound experience comes together through four key decisions, each designed to give businesses the right control without adding complexity.

Compose

Write or select a message template.

Target

Choose the audience and set conditions.

Schedule

Define when and how often to send.

Route

Direct replies to a team, rule or bot.

06 / From message to conversation

The message is the beginning.

Outbound messages don’t end at delivery. Responses flow back into SalesIQ, where bots or teams can continue the conversation.

07 / The outcome

“Different needs. Different ways to reach out. One connected experience.”

Outbound Messages gives businesses a flexible, controlled way to start conversations, from timely notifications to large-scale broadcasts, all within SalesIQ.

In the shipped product · From public Zoho documentation

What the feature does today

WhatsApp broadcast

Schedule bulk WhatsApp messages from pre-approved templates.

Sending preferences

Start date, end schedule and frequency, to avoid over-messaging.

Website and app broadcasts

One message to many leads and contacts, shown in the chat window, on a recurring schedule.

Outbound reports

Broadcast and proactive results read from a single dashboard.
