01 / Why a mascot

A portfolio is read alone. Sunny makes it feel like someone is home.

Most visitors skim. A character that notices them gives the site a voice, makes the small details discoverable, and turns a quiet visit into a short conversation.

Notices you

Reacts to scrolling, idling, leaving the tab, a right-click, or being picked up.

Knows the page

Comments on the section you’re reading and points to the work behind it.

Answers questions

The Ask panel answers questions about my work, backed by a small knowledge file.

02 / Emotions

Twenty faces, one yellow ball.

Emotions are quick faces that last a second or two. Behind them sits a mood that lasts longer and tints how Sunny idles, which acts it picks and what it says.

Behind the faces sit 11 longer moods (neutral, happy, curious, excited, proud, shy, sleepy, relaxed, focused, dizzy, confused) that tint how Sunny idles.

03 / Acts

Twenty-one things to do while you read.

When nobody is interacting, Sunny picks an act that fits its mood. Some come with props, a sound and a line at the end, and anything you do interrupts them. Hover a tile to play it, click to make Sunny do it.

Everyday: wave, hop, peek, stretch, dance, walk, skip, sleep · Hobbies: cycle, swim, skate, fish, yoga, read, sing, selfie, joker · Off-duty: drink, beer, drunk, smoke

04 / What Sunny notices

Small signals, small reactions.

Drag and throw

Pick Sunny up and let go; it lands, wobbles and has opinions.

Idle time

Leave it alone and it gets bored, then sleepy, then naps.

Time of day

Morning, evening and night change the greetings and the energy.

Reading

Scrolling and section changes shape what it comments on.

Leaving the tab

Notices you went away, and says so when you come back.

Right-click

Puts on sunglasses. Too cool for context menus.

Keyboard

Responds to keys and shortcuts on the site.

Nap button

A small “zz” button lets visitors send Sunny to sleep.

07 / Design decisions

A mascot that never gets in the way.

01

Page first, Sunny second.

Sunny mounts after the page has loaded, in an idle slot, so the first paint never waits for the mascot.

02

Chatter is rationed.

Ambient lines are limited, and anything the visitor does takes priority over an act already running.

03

Motion is optional.

With reduced motion on, acts are switched off and Sunny stays calm. Visitors can also send it to nap.

08 / Takeaway

“Personality is a detail, repeated.”

No single blink or line makes Sunny feel alive. Hundreds of small, well-timed ones do, and each one has to know when to stay quiet.
