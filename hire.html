03

Questions I get asked

Straight answers, so the first call can go deeper.

Do you code?

Enough to ship this site and to speak the language of the people who build my designs. HTML and CSS by hand, JavaScript with help from Claude. On the product I work in Figma variables and Code Connect so every component maps to its real code, and I use Figma MCP to generate production components from the library. I am not a front-end engineer and I do not pretend to be one.

Have you led a team?

I have led the design direction, not the people. I own the SalesIQ design system and the design reviews across a five-engineer team, set the product design practices, and align product managers and engineers on what ships. No direct reports yet, and a lead role where that changes is exactly what I am looking for.

What tools do you use?

Figma every day: variables and modes, Code Connect, Dev Mode and Figma MCP. Claude and ChatGPT for critique, research synthesis and design-to-code. Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and Spline for visuals and motion. HTML and CSS for prototypes and this site.

What roles are you looking for?

Lead or staff product designer, or a senior role that owns a design system. Enterprise or B2B SaaS, with a team that ships. I am most useful where design and engineering need one source of truth.

What is your notice period?

30 days.

Where do you work from?

Chennai, India, on IST. I am open to on-site or hybrid in Chennai, remote for an Indian or international team with IST overlap, and relocating for the right role.

Can I see the real product?

Most of my work is under NDA with Zoho. The case studies recreate every screen with sample data, and I walk through the full Figma files live on a call, or share them with a password on request.

How fast do you reply?

Within a day, usually the same day.
