Sunny, the prototype guide.

Sunny lives in the bottom-right corner of every page. Left alone, it picks something to do every few seconds. Use the buttons to trigger any act or face. Move your pointer, click, double-click, right-click, drag it along the edge, scroll fast, switch the theme, or leave it alone for a while.

What triggers what on the real pages: arrival wave and a greeting · waves when your pointer comes near · click: smile, blush and one little chirp · double-click: hop and spin · triple-click: annoyed · right-click: sunglasses · drag: carried along the edge, lands with a squash · it also smokes, drinks, sings, reads, does yoga, skates, fishes, takes selfies and skips rope on its own · fast scroll: braces · page bottom: dance and a message · theme switch: sunglasses in light, cosy in dark · pointer leaves the window: sad, returns: happy · 40 s without input: sleeps until you move · the small “zz” on hover puts Sunny to bed for the session. Sound: on your first click, tap or key press Sunny welcomes you with a chime (browsers block audio before that). Every click plays the smile chirp. Every emotion has its own small sound (gasp, hmm, ting, aww, giggle, wobble, sigh…). The acts have quiet cues (bike bell, splash, gulps and hiccups, camera shutter, ta-da, swoosh on fast scrolls, soft snore). The buttons above switch sound and cues off; choices are remembered in this browser only.
