What AI does, what I decide

AI handles

  • First drafts and alternatives
  • Execution (UI, code, documentation)
  • Exploration (variants, edge cases)

I own

  • The problem and direction
  • Visual and system decisions
  • What ships (the final call)

Disclose AI
