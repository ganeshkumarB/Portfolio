04

Three decisions that shaped it

01

Show the change, not just the number

Every KPI carries its change against the previous period, green or red, so a glance tells you if things got better.

02

One time axis for every chart

Charts on a page share the same hours, so you can scan down and line up a spike in chats with a dip in response.

03

The rows are always one scroll away

Each report ends in the chats behind the numbers, with export and email, so nobody has to ask where a figure came from.

06

What I learned

“Every chart should answer one question.”

Once reports were grouped by the question an admin asks, the navigation became obvious, and choosing the right chart got much easier.
