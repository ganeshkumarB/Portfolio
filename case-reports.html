01 / Context

Reports were valuable, but hard to navigate.

The existing reports provided useful data, but the experience felt fragmented, visually noisy and inconsistent across different report types.

Articles report

Consolidated portal-level business reports can be viewed, emailed and saved as a document, and department usage can be filtered for any given range.

02 / The challenge

How can we make complex operational data easier to scan, compare and explore?

  • Bring consistencyacross different report types.
  • Make key insightsfaster to understand.
  • Help teams go froman overview to deeper analysis, effortlessly.

03 / The approach

A clearer structure for deeper insights.

I reimagined the reporting experience around a simple, consistent structure: overview, trends, distribution and detailed records.

Overview

Key metrics at a glance.

Trends

04 / The new experience

From high-level performance to conversation details.

A unified reporting experience with clear hierarchy, consistent visual patterns, and everything teams need to understand, monitor and improve customer interactions.
