04 / Story

      
I make complex things feel simple.

      
I started in visual design, where I learned to care about the details. Over time, I moved into product design, looking beyond screens to understand the problems people actually face.

      
Today, I work across product experiences, design systems, and the space between design and code. I enjoy making complicated things simpler, building systems that scale, and exploring how AI can change the way we design and build.

      
I like understanding how things work, simplifying complexity, and exploring how design and AI can help build better experiences.
