06 / Takeaway

Feedback is more than a score.

It is the context that makes it meaningful.

In the shipped product · From public Zoho documentation

What the feature does today

After every chat

Visitors are shown a feedback form when a chat ends.

Feedback module

Responses are listed newest first, each opening a detailed chat review.

Rating filters and export

Filter by Happy, Neutral or Sad ratings, and export the results.

Zoho Survey

Send surveys through chat and map answers to visitor information.
