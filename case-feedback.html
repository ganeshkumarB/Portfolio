Stars

03

A different reply for every rating

Happy visitors are invited to share. Unhappy ones are offered help, before they leave.

Trigger

Visitor rates the chat

Each rating starts its own follow-up, set up without code.

04

Three decisions that shaped it

01

One tap first

The rating is a single tap and the comment is optional, so answering costs the visitor almost nothing.

02

Reply to how they feel

Each rating can lead somewhere different, so a bad experience gets help instead of a generic thank-you.

03

A survey when you need more

For deeper questions, a Zoho Survey opens inside the conversation and maps answers to the visitor.

05

What I learned

“The easiest question gets the most answers.”

Keeping the first step to a single tap mattered more than any question that came after it.
