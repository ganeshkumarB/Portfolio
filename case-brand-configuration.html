05 / The decisions

Three decisions that shaped it.

01

The brand is the home, not the channel.

A single place to manage the brand, with clear entry points for each channel.

02

Shared first, channel second.

Common details like business hours, departments and security are set once and applied everywhere.

03

Room for the next channel.

A structure that can grow: new channels are added without redesigning the experience.

06 / What changed

What changed.

A more focused, flexible and scalable brand configuration experience.

Developer help

From multiple dependencies to a self-service setup.

Self-service

Business and channel settings in one place, with clear guidance.

Channels in one place

Website, SDK and WhatsApp, managed under a single brand.

07 / The takeaway

“Settings are a place, not a list.”

Giving each brand one home, with every channel visible on it, did more for self-service than any single new setting.
