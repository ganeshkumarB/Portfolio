04 — Your thinking

Three decisions that shaped it

01

The brand is the home, not the channel

You open a brand, not a channel. Its name, logo and business details sit at the top of every page.

02

Shared first, channel second

Departments, hours and language are set once for the brand, so adding a channel only asks for what is new about it.

03

Room for the next channel

New channels slot in as one more card, so channels like Line arrive without changing the page.

07 — Reflection

What I learned

“Settings are a place, not a list.”

Giving each brand one home, with every channel visible on it, did more for self-service than any single new setting.
